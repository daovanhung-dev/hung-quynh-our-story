# Museum asset attribution

The visitor GLB models and textures in `kenney/blocky-characters` come from
[Kenney's Blocky Characters](https://www.kenney.nl/assets/blocky-characters),
distributed under [Creative Commons CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The museum architecture and props are procedural scene geometry. The asset
manifest keeps their CC0 source metadata so they can be replaced by local
CC0 models later without changing the scene API.
